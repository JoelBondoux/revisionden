# 0004: Pricing plan for a public release

- Status: accepted (product owner, 2026-11-25)
- Date: 2026-11-25

## Context

The product owner intends to publish RevisionDen. The competitor research
(`docs/research/competitors-2026-11.md`) found paid UK revision at about £4–£25 a month per child
and almost no real family plans.

## Decision

- Free app; Premium per child per month: £9.99, then £4.99, then £2.99, then free from the 4th
  child. Annual plan (about £79.99 for the first child), 14-day free trial, prices include VAT,
  web-only sales.
- Free/Premium split and fair-selling rules as in `docs/PRICING.md`: learning, safety and
  accessibility stay free; only parents see offers; rewards are never sold; no adverts.
- Free Premium for free school meals families: choice of scheme and date only (no reference
  numbers), declaration, soft checks, rare real spot checks with proof deleted at once; school and
  charity codes later.

## Consequences

- New roadmap workstream $ (payments and Premium gating). The privacy notice and terms need updating
  before anything is charged.
