## Fair selling (Children's Code)

- Only parents see upgrade offers and prices, in the parent area. Students never see adverts for
  Premium, locked-feature teasers aimed at them, or prompts to ask a parent to pay.
- Rewards, XP, items and titles can never be bought. No adverts, ever.
- When a Premium feature is not included, the student sees a neutral line ("Your family plan doesn't
  include this yet"), never a sales message.
- Cancelling is as easy as subscribing; the family keeps everything earned and all free features.

## Free school meals (free Premium)

Families whose child gets free school meals, or who get a qualifying benefit, get Premium free for
every child in the family.

- **Claim:** a parent chooses which applies from a short list: "My child gets free school meals",
  "I get Universal Credit", or "I get another qualifying benefit". **We store only that choice and
  the date**, never a reference or National Insurance number, document or amount.
- **Declaration:** "I confirm this is true. RevisionDen may occasionally ask for proof, and misuse
  ends the free place."
- **Soft checks:**
  - the family must have used the free version for at least 2 weeks before claiming;
  - one free place per payment card (if one was ever added) and per parent sign-in;
  - the claim renews each September with a fresh declaration.
- **Spot checks (real, rare):** a small random sample of free places are asked, by email to the
  parent's sign-in address, to show a letter (for example the school's free school meals letter or
  a benefit statement). The operator looks, records only "checked on <date>", and deletes the
  email and attachment straight away. No reply in 30 days ends the free place, with a reminder first.
- **Later:** schools, councils and youth charities can be given codes for families they already
  know qualify (no proof reaches us). See the roadmap.
- The privacy notice must describe the claim (choice and date only) and the spot-check process
  before this goes live.
