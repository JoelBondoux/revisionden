# Privacy notice for young people

Version: 1.0
Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

## In plain English

This is for you if you are a student using RevisionDen. It explains what happens to your
information. Your parent or carer has a longer version, the [privacy notice](privacy-notice.md).
If the two ever seem different, the longer one is the one that counts.

- **We don't know who you are.** You use a nickname, not your real name. We never ask for your
  name, birthday, address, phone number, email, school or photos.
- **What we keep:** your nickname, your avatar, your school year, your subjects, and your
  revision: which questions you did, if you got them right, how long you took, your XP, streaks,
  rewards, flashcards and study plan.
- **Why:** so the app can give you the right questions, help you with your weak spots and plan
  your revision. That's it. No adverts. We never sell your information.
- **Your parent can see it.** Your parent or carer can see your profile, your subjects, your AI
  use and anything you send us as feedback, and soon your progress too.
- **AI marking.** Some written answers are marked with help from an AI. We check your answer
  first and stop it being sent if it has your nickname, an email or a phone number in it. Please
  don't write your name or personal details in answers.
- **AI marks are a guide**, not your teacher's mark.
- **Reminders** are off until you turn them on. You can turn them off any time.
- **You have rights.** You can ask to see your information, fix it or delete it. You can ask your
  parent, or contact us yourself at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
- **Not happy?** Tell us, or tell the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), who make sure
  companies follow the law: ico.org.uk.

## Full text

### Who runs RevisionDen

RevisionDen is run by {{OPERATOR_NAME}}. We are the "controller", which means we decide how your
information is used and we are responsible for looking after it. Contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}
or by post at {{POSTAL_ADDRESS}}.

### How your account works

Your parent or carer set up your profile. You don't have an email or password with us. Your
device is linked with a QR code, and then you unlock the app with a passkey, which is your
device's own fingerprint, face or PIN check. We never see your fingerprint or face: your device
checks it and only tells us "yes, it's them".

### What we keep about you

- **Your profile:** your nickname, your avatar (saved as a short code) and your school year.
- **Your courses:** your subjects, exam boards and tiers.
- **Your devices:** a code that lets your passkey work, and when the device was linked and last
  used.
- **Your revision:** the questions you answered, if you got them right, your score, how long you
  took, your XP, streaks, skip days, gift days, rewards, flashcards (including the words on any
  cards you make yourself), your study plan and any exam or mock dates and results that are
  entered.
- **Reminders:** if you turn them on, an address your browser gives us so we can send them.
- **AI use:** how much AI marking you used this month (just numbers, never your answers).
- **Feedback:** anything you choose to send us.

We do **not** keep the words of your answers to questions. We use them to mark the question,
then only keep the result.

### What we never collect

Your real name, birthday, address, phone number, email, photos, location, contacts or school
name.

### Why we use it

To run the revision app: choosing the right questions, marking them, finding your weak spots,
planning revision, and keeping track of your streaks and rewards. The law calls our reason
"legitimate interests". It means we have a good reason, and we have thought about whether it
is fair to you. Because you are under 18, your interests come first.

We do not use your information for adverts, and we don't sell it or give it to anyone for their
own use.

### The app changes as you learn

The app works out which topics you find hard and gives you more of those. It also changes how
often you see your results. A computer does this automatically, but it doesn't make any decision
that really affects your life, like an exam grade. Your real grades come from your exams and your
teachers.

### AI marking

When a written answer can't be marked by simple rules, the question, the topic, your answer and
the official mark scheme are sent to an AI service (at the moment Claude, made by Anthropic).
Before anything is sent we check it, and stop it if it contains your nickname, your school year,
an email address or a phone number. The AI is never told who you are. Some AI companies are in
the United States, so your answer may be processed there.

Every AI mark says: "Marked by AI. This is a guide to help you improve, not your teacher's mark."
If the AI isn't sure, it says "Check with your teacher" instead of guessing. See
[AI and marking](ai-and-marking.md).

### Photos of homework (coming later)

Reading photos of homework isn't available yet. When it is: **cover your name and your school's
name before you take the photo.** The photo is read, you check what was read, and the photo is
deleted straight away (and always within 24 hours).

### Who can see your information

- **Your parent or carer**, who can see your profile, subjects, AI use and feedback, and (soon)
  your progress.
- **The companies that run the app for us**, like the hosting company and the database, but only
  what each one needs to do its job. They're listed in [services we use](processors.md).
- Nobody else. There's no chat and no way for other students to see your work.

### How long we keep it

Until your parent deletes your profile, which they can do at any time. Some things are deleted
sooner. See [how long we keep data](data-retention.md).

### Your rights

You have the right to:

- know what we do with your information (this page);
- see your information and get a copy;
- fix anything that is wrong;
- ask us to delete it;
- ask us to stop using it, or object to how we use it;
- get your information in a file you can take elsewhere.

You can ask your parent or carer to help, or you can contact us yourself at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
If you are old enough to understand what you are asking, we will listen to you and reply to you.
We will reply within a month.

### If you're not happy

Tell us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office
(ICO), which makes sure organisations follow data protection law: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or
0303 123 1113. You can ask a trusted adult to help you.

### Changes

If we change how we use your information, we'll show you a short note in the app.
