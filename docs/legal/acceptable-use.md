# Community rules

Version: 1.1
Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

## In plain English

These rules keep RevisionDen safe and fair for everyone.

- **Pick a nickname, not your name.** No real names, no rude words, nothing that would upset
  someone, and nothing that pretends to be someone else.
- **Keep personal details out.** Don't put your name, school, address, phone number, email or
  anything private into answers, flashcards or feedback.
- **There is no chat.** Students can't message each other in RevisionDen.
- **Friends (coming later)** will only see your nickname, avatar and XP, and a parent has to
  approve each friend.
- **Gift days** are for friends. Gifting is a kind thing to do, not something to pressure anyone
  about.
- **Play fair.** Don't try to break the app, cheat XP or use someone else's device.
- **Be kind in feedback.** Tell us what's wrong; don't be rude.
- If you see something wrong in a question, use "Report a problem".
- If the summary and the full text seem to differ, the full text is the one that counts.

## Full text

### 1. Who these rules apply to

1.1 These rules apply to everyone who uses RevisionDen: parents, carers and students. They form
part of the [terms of use](terms-of-use.md). {{OPERATOR_NAME}} may update them; material changes
are shown in the app.

### 2. Nicknames and avatars

2.1 A nickname must be 2 to 16 letters, numbers or underscores and is checked against a list of
words that are not allowed. It must not be a real name (your own or anyone else's), must not
include a school name, contact details or anything offensive, and must not impersonate another
person.

2.2 Avatars are built from the app's own pieces; no photos can be uploaded as an avatar.

### 3. Personal details

3.1 Do not enter personal details (names, schools, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses,
social media names or anything private about yourself or others) in answers, in your own
flashcards or in feedback.

3.2 Answers sent for AI marking and feedback are checked automatically and refused if they contain
an email address, a UK mobile phone number or a nickname from your family. Flashcards you write
yourself are not checked automatically yet, so please take extra care.

3.3 Do not include health information or other sensitive information about anyone.

### 4. Communication between students

4.1 There is no chat and no free text between students.

4.2 **Friends** are not available yet. When they are: friends will see only each other's nickname,
avatar and XP; adding a friend will need an invite code and a parent's approval; and either family
will be able to remove the friendship.

4.3 **Gift days.** Students can give a gift day (earned with streak badges) to a friend, which
arrives as a skip day. Gifts must never be asked for in return for anything, and pressure to gift
is not allowed.

### 5. Feedback and reports

5.1 Feedback text must be about the app and must not be abusive, threatening or offensive.

5.2 Mistakes in a question should be reported with "Report a problem" on the question. A report
stores only the question, its version and a reason chosen from a list.

5.3 Please do not send false reports on purpose: every report hides the question from all
students until it is checked.

### 6. Fair play and security

6.1 Do not:

- try to get around the app's security, rate limits or AI spending limit;
- try to see another family's data;
- use automated tools to answer questions or earn XP;
- use another student's profile or device, or link a device that is not yours;
- try to make the AI produce content that is harmful, or that is not about the question.

  6.2 If you find a security problem, please tell us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and do not share it.

### 7. What happens if the rules are broken

7.1 Depending on how serious it is, we may remove a nickname or text, reset XP or rewards gained
unfairly, or suspend a profile or family account, as set out in the
[terms of use](terms-of-use.md). We will normally tell the parent first and explain why.

### 8. Getting help

8.1 If anything in the app upsets or worries you, tell a parent, carer or teacher, and let us know
at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
