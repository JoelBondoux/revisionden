# Accessibility statement

Version: 1.1
Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

## In plain English

- We want everyone to be able to use RevisionDen, including people who use a screen reader, a
  keyboard, zoom or other tools.
- We aim to meet the international standard WCAG 2.2 at level AA.
- We haven't fully tested the whole app yet, so there may be problems. Some are listed below.
- If something is hard to use, tell us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we'll try to fix it or help
  another way.
- If the summary and the full text seem to differ, the full text is the one that counts.

## Full text

### 1. Our commitment

1.1 {{OPERATOR_NAME}} wants RevisionDen to be usable by as many people as possible. Our target is
the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2, level AA.

### 2. What we do

- Every text and background colour pair is checked in our tests for a contrast ratio of at least
  4.5 to 1.
- The app respects the device's "reduce motion" setting (for example, the flashcard flip).
- Charts are planned with a table view, so the information is not only in a picture.
- Sound is never the only signal: every sound has a matching visual.
- Policy pages work without signing in and can be printed.
- The app can be installed and works with the browser's own zoom and text size.
- The interface language is set to British English so screen readers read it correctly.

### 3. How accessible the app is

3.1 We believe the app partly meets WCAG 2.2 AA. It has not yet been fully tested with automated
accessibility tools across every screen, or by people using assistive technology.

3.2 Known gaps and work still to do:

- automated accessibility testing of every screen in our end-to-end tests is planned but not
  built yet;
- testing with screen readers (for example VoiceOver and NVDA) is still to do;
- maths notation in questions and working may not always be read out clearly by screen readers;
- the topic map is a visual hexagon layout; we have not yet confirmed it is fully usable by
  keyboard and screen reader alone;
- timed papers (when added) will need a way to extend time.

### 4. Feedback and contact

4.1 If you find a problem, or need information in a different format, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or
use **Report a bug** in the app. Tell us the screen and what you were trying to do. We will reply as
soon as we can.

### 5. Preparation of this statement

5.1 This statement was prepared by the team building the app, based on our own checks. It has not
yet been verified by an independent audit. We will review it whenever we make a significant change
and at least once a year.
