# Pearson's terms for quoting its specifications (November 2026)

> Research document, checked 2026-11-27 by workstream S4 at the lead's request, before any A-Level
> Maths or Further Maths (Pearson Edexcel 9MA0 / 9FM0) item is written. The question: what do
> Pearson's published terms allow when we quote specification text in our citations, and what do
> they forbid, including anything about AI? Official Pearson pages only, quoted word for word. This
> is a record of what the terms say, not legal advice. No personal data.

## Summary

- **Pearson does not grant permission for what we do.** Its copyright policy says Edexcel
  materials, "This includes specifications", "may not be copied or made available electronically
  in whole or in part without permission from Pearson" (section 2.1).
- **The exceptions it lists are for exam centres only.** They cover copying inside a centre, and
  centre intranets that "cannot be accessed publicly". RevisionDen is not a centre, and this
  repository is public. No listed exception covers quoting specification text in a public app or
  repository.
- **Pearson's website terms reserve AI and text and data mining rights.** The `www.pearson.com`
  terms say "All rights reserved, including those for text and data mining and training of
  artificial intelligence and similar technologies", and allow use "for personal, non-commercial
  and class room use only" (section 3). These are the `www.pearson.com` site terms; the
  qualifications site links to them as its "Legal notice" and has no terms page of its own.
- **The qualifications pages and specifications have no AI clause of their own.** The copyright
  policy, the publication policy and the three specifications do not mention AI, machine learning
  or text and data mining.
- **What this decides.** On Pearson's own published terms, quoting 9MA0 / 9FM0 specification text
  in our citations needs either written permission from Pearson or a legal view that a statutory
  exception applies. The website terms make this stronger, not weaker: no Pearson text should be
  quoted without one of the two. The same question already applies to the GCSE 1MA1 pilot (section 4). The
  owner should decide before an A-Level Maths content team starts.

## 2. What the terms say (word for word)

### 2.1 Pearson copyright policy

> "All materials published by Edexcel are protected by copyright legislation and may not be copied
> or made available electronically in whole or in part without permission from Pearson. This
> includes specifications, schemes of work, past question papers and mark schemes, and all other
> materials."

> "We do give permission for our centres to access and copy our materials in certain contexts,
> including those related to examinations. These circumstances are detailed below."

Past papers and mark schemes, for "an approved examination centre":

> "You only use photocopies and print-outs from web downloads or CD-ROMs for practice
> examinations or revision or for teaching purposes within the centre."

> "You don't charge students or anyone else for any of these materials."

Intranets and microsites:

> "You may save copies of Edexcel materials onto your centre's intranet or microsite provided you
> observe the following conditions:"

> "These intranets or microsites are for use by the centre community only and cannot be accessed
> publicly."

> "Materials are used for the purposes of instruction only."

> "Materials are not copied for a different use outside the centre."

> "Materials are not offered for sale in any format."

> "The integrity of the original materials is maintained - for example, meanings are not changed
> and items remain unabridged."

Permissions contact:

> "You can contact the Copyright Manager directly by email at copyrightPQS@pearson.com."

### 2.2 Qualification and Assessment publication policy (version 2.3)

Scope (section 1.1) covers "Level 3 Advanced GCEs", which includes 9MA0 and 9FM0.

> "4.1 The current specifications for all regulated and self-regulated qualifications covered by
> this policy are available to download free of charge from the relevant qualification pages on
> our website."

> "7.1 All materials published by Pearson are protected by copyright legislation. Pearson centres
> are permitted to access and use these materials for some specific purposes and in defined
> contexts. Full details are set out in Pearson’s copyright policy."

> "10.1 Queries relating to copyright should be directed to the Copyright Manager at
> copyright@pearson.com."

Free download is not the same as permission to republish. The policy grants no reuse beyond the
copyright policy.

### 2.3 The specifications themselves

Each specification's back page says only this, with no further licence, permission or AI clause:

- 9MA0 Issue 4 (page 73 of the PDF): "All the material in this publication is copyright © Pearson
  Education Limited 2020"
- 9FM0 Issue 4 (page 91 of the PDF): "All the material in this publication is copyright © Pearson
  Education Limited 2023"
- 1MA1 Issue 2 (pilot): "All the material in this publication is copyright © Pearson Education
  Limited 2015"

The only matches for "artificial" in 9FM0 are "artificial variables" in Decision Mathematics, which
is maths content, not a term of use.

### 2.4 Injunction (for completeness)

Pearson holds a High Court injunction, granted after a hearing on 30 April 2026, that "prohibits
anyone in the United Kingdom from attempting to obtain, transfer, publish or sell examination papers
or associated materials". It is about live exam papers obtained in advance. RevisionDen writes its
own questions, and past papers are linked, not copied (`docs/CONTENT_PLAN.md`), so it does not
apply to what we do.

"Our Rights in Content; Limited Licence":

> "All materials on or available through this Site is the copyright of Pearson and its group
> companies and its licensors, unless otherwise stated. All rights are reserved. Users are
> permitted to view, print and download the material for personal, non-commercial and class room
> use only but in so doing are not permitted to remove or amend any trademark, copyright or other
> proprietary notice. 'Class room use' means non-commercial uses by a qualified teacher during a
> class of students registered in a qualified educational institution."

### 3.3 What this adds

- **Text and data mining and AI training are reserved** on `www.pearson.com`. The terms grant no
  licence for either.
- **The licence is narrow.** Viewing, printing and downloading for "personal, non-commercial and
  class room use only". Republishing in a public repository or app is not among them.
- **Scope is not certain.** The terms say "this Site". The specifications are served from
  `qualifications.pearson.com`, which links to these terms as its legal notice but does not say
  they apply to it. Whether they cover the specifications is part of the legal question in
  section 4.
- **Not read:** any Pearson Acceptable Use Policy that search extracts mention. It was not opened
  and is not relied on here.

## 4. What this means for RevisionDen

- **Our use.** Every item's citation quotes a short, located excerpt of the specification
  (`docs/VERIFICATION.md`). The items sit in this public repository and are served in the app. On
  Pearson's published terms, that is making part of a specification "available electronically",
  and Pearson's policy grants permission only to centres, in closed settings.
- **What the terms do not say.** They do not mention statutory copyright exceptions, such as
  quotation. Whether such an exception covers short located citations is a legal question. This
  note does not answer it, and nothing here should be read as permission.
- **Same question for the pilot.** The GCSE 1MA1 items already quote the 1MA1 specification in
  this public repository. Any decision for 9MA0 / 9FM0 also applies there. The open entry in
  `project_memory/operations/contradictions.md` ("AQA's AI clause and our content pipeline") now
  points here for Pearson.
- **Options for the owner** (the owner decides; none is chosen here):
  1. Write to the Copyright Manager (copyrightPQS@pearson.com, or copyright@pearson.com in the
     publication policy). Ask for permission to quote short, located specification excerpts in
     citations, and ask whether Pearson's AI terms apply to AI-assisted writing of original
     questions.
  2. Get a legal view on whether short, located quotations fall under a statutory exception.
  3. Cite by location only: specification code, issue and page, with no quoted text. Our quote
     check would then need a different design, because today it requires a quote
     (`docs/VERIFICATION.md`).
- **Until then:** no new Pearson specification quotes are added, and existing items stay `draft`.
  These findings do not support starting A-Level Maths items that quote 9MA0 or 9FM0 text.
