# Changelog

All notable changes are recorded here. Versions use the format `xx.xx.xxx` (major.minor.patch).
Every push to `main` bumps the version automatically; add entries under **Unreleased** in each PR.

## [Unreleased]

### What's new

- The parent stats page now shows each child's AI marking allowance as a percentage used this month and the date it resets, with no money figures.
- On the parent stats page you can now add, edit and remove your child's rewards, and see every reward with its progress.
- New "Add RevisionDen to this device" button on your topic map and on the parent home. On Chrome, Edge and Android it installs the app in one tap; on an iPhone or iPad it shows the three steps. Tap "Not now" to hide it for 30 days.
- Students: when RevisionDen asks you to unlock (after 12 hours), tap "I'm a student: unlock with my passkey" and use your face, fingerprint or screen lock. You go straight back to where you were. No need to ask a parent for a new QR code.
- New Appearance settings: choose a dark theme (or let it follow your device), colour-blind friendly colours (blue for right, orange for wrong), and a subtle doodle background with Pip and maths sketches that you can switch off.
- No more plain underlined links: Settings, the parent home, "Who's revising?", About, Get help, policies and feedback now use tappable cards and buttons with an icon and a short description. Links inside the policies stay in the text but are bold and coloured, so they are easy to spot.
- New parents now see the Terms of use and Privacy notice as easy-to-read cards before they start: the 4 most important points first, the rest and the full text one tap away.
- When RevisionDen support replies to your feedback or bug report, you'll see the reply right under your message.
- Coming soon: a public RevisionDen website at revisionden.com explaining how it works for parents and young people, with the planned prices and all the policies. The app itself will move to app.revisionden.com.
- "Remember this?" questions are now completely safe: getting one wrong never breaks your combo.
- New 5-a-day: a quick mix of 5 questions from topics you've already practised, at the top of your topic map until you've done it. It takes about 5–8 minutes and counts towards your daily goal and streak like any lesson.
- Six more hidden reward tracks to discover, like Comeback Kid (turn a weak topic into a strong one) and Explorer (try every kind of practice). Each stays secret until you earn its first step.
- Target grades: for each subject, a parent can set a target grade on the stats dashboard and your child can set their own on My stats. You both see both, as goals to reach for (GCSE Maths for now; more subjects as their grade scales are checked).
- Two parents: invite a second parent from Parent home with a code or link. You both get the same control, a family activity log shows who changed what (as Parent 1 or Parent 2), and you're told about important changes. Deleting the family account needs both of you.
- Learn it first: a button at the start of a topic lesson opens free lessons from Oak National Academy for that topic in a new tab. It shows up on each GCSE Maths topic once its link has been checked.
- Spotted a bug? Tap the little bug button in the corner to tell us. If we confirm it, you earn +50 XP. You can hide the button in Settings.
- Read aloud: tap the speaker button to hear a question, the feedback and explanation, or a flashcard, with your device's own voice. Maths is read properly ("x squared"). Turn it on or off and change the speed in Settings.
- Remember this? A lesson on a topic you've practised before starts with 2 quick questions from earlier lessons. There's no penalty: anything you miss becomes a flashcard, and you can skip them.
- Reading comfort in Settings: a dyslexia-friendly font, larger text, more space between lines and letters, and a cream or blue background tint. Your choice is remembered on this device and used on every screen.
- Earn a reward while answering questions or reviewing flashcards and you'll see it (and hear its sound) straight away, not only on the Rewards page.
- Get help now lists Kooth for feeling low (free in some parts of the UK) and Coram Kidscape for bullying. Parents also see its Parent Advice Line (phone, WhatsApp or email) and the Anti-Bullying Alliance.
- The right-answer chime now climbs a little higher with each right answer in a row, up to six in a row.
- A daily study limit: students see how many minutes are left today, and practice past the limit says "Great work today. See you tomorrow" (no XP after that). Parents set the limit for school days and other days, and the app suggests small changes once a week that you can accept, change or dismiss.
- On the test site (dev.revisionden.com) you can now try the first practice questions while they are still being checked; they are marked "DRAFT CONTENT".
- The topic map, rewards and stats now move a little: cards slide in, hexagons pop up and buttons bounce. New rewards show how special they are, what you get and exactly why you earned it.
- Lessons have a new game feel: a dark arena with big coloured answer buttons, a burst of colour and floating XP for right answers, a COMBO badge for answers in a row, Pip cheering you on, and a "Lesson complete" celebration with stars and confetti, then Next mission. Turn on reduced motion on your device for a calmer version.
- When something goes wrong, tap "Send error report" to tell us in one tap. It's anonymous: no names, nothing you typed. We'll get on it.
- Coming soon: a daily 5-a-day, quick recall checks, links to free lessons for each topic, read aloud, and a dyslexia-friendly reading mode.
- Coming soon: build your own title from words you unlock, like "Cosmic Scholar".
- New "Get help" page: UK support services by topic (Childline, Samaritans, Shout, NHS 111 and more), with tap-to-call and tap-to-text. It is private (nothing about it is recorded or shown to parents), works offline and has a Quick exit button. Find it in Settings or from the heart in the footer.
- Coming soon: invite a second parent to share control of the family.
- Coming soon: more hidden reward tracks to discover.
- Combo bonus: from your 3rd right answer in a row, each right answer earns 5 extra XP. In the starting test, the bonus is added when you see your results.
- Your topic map now shows your real progress, and tapping a topic or the next mission starts a lesson.
- Coming soon: a bug button on every screen. Find a real bug and earn bonus XP and secret Bug Hunter rewards.
- Secret achievements: 24 hidden ones to find, shown as "???" until you earn one, with a few vague hints. Earning one reveals it with a celebration.
- Bug Hunter: your first confirmed bug reveals a hidden track with a badge, a title, a magnifying glass, a circuit-board frame and more, and the bonus XP counts towards your level.
- Coming later: timed exam practice. Sit a full paper against the clock, then see your marks, your likely grade and how to pace yourself better.
- The privacy notice for students is now much easier to read: a short version on six cards, the key points of each part up front, and "Read more" when you want the detail.
- Parents get a notification on their phone when a child reaches a reward target you set. Turn on "Reward news" on the new parent home.
- New parent home: one place for the stats dashboard, rewards you set, reminder times, family feedback, settings and policies. Find it from the profile picker or Settings.
- When you're signed out, About, Policies and Feedback show a simple header (Sign in, Policies, About) instead of the student menu.
- Parents can download all their family's data as one file, from the family list.
- Accounts nobody uses for 12 months are now deleted automatically; the parent area shows the date, and warns you if you come back after a long break.
- Your own flashcards can't hold email addresses, phone numbers or family nicknames, to keep personal details out.
- After an update, the app now shows "A new version of RevisionDen is ready" with a Refresh button, so you're never stuck on an old version.
- On "Who's revising?", tapping a child now opens your view of them (their stats), with quick links to the rewards you set and to link their device. Students use the app on their own device.
- Need help? Email help@revisionden.com. You'll find it on the About page and the feedback screen.
- Signing in with Google now really signs you in, and "Who's revising?" shows your children instead of "Something went wrong". New families go straight to adding a child. If you're signed out, any page shows the sign-in buttons and brings you back where you were. You can sign out from the welcome page or "Who's revising?".
- When something does go wrong, the app shows a short code (like RD-7K2M9Q). Send it to us and we can find what happened.
- Reward sounds now grow with the reward: a bright chime for common rewards up to a short fanfare for legendary ones, with lots of variety, a different sound for each school year and special fanfares for finishing GCSEs and A-Levels. Tap to skip a long one, or try them all in Settings.
- RevisionDen is now online for testing at dev.revisionden.com and staging.revisionden.com, with the app version shown at the bottom of every page.
- Flashcards now give 20 XP for finishing a session of at least 5 cards (up to twice a day), not for each card, so there's no point tapping "Good" on everything. Cards you mark "Again" come back before the session ends.
- New stats page for students (XP, accuracy, streak and mastery by week, term or year) and a stats dashboard for parents.
- Parents can set their own rewards, like a cinema trip for a 30-day streak, and mark them as given. Students see them under "From home".
- Sound effects: soft, original sounds for right and wrong answers, finishing a lesson, meeting your daily goal, flashcards and the starting test. Mute them from the speaker button at the top, or change the volume and tap sounds in Settings.
- Read our privacy notice, terms and other policies in plain English, at any time, even signed out.
- New About page shows the app version and what's new.
- Send us a bug, an idea or something confusing from Settings, and see what happens to it.

### Fixed

- Fact-check stage (S2, Codex findings on #116): a cited quote now counts only if the judge confirms it states the fact (not just its topic), otherwise the fact needs the engine proof and blind statement; and each `content:model-check` stage sees the checks recorded earlier in the same run, so one provider can't give both the blind answer and the blind statement.
- Multiple choice reviews (S2, cross-lane V): the adversarial review prompt now says option numbers count from 0 (also `distractor:N`) and shows the correct option's text, so the reviewer no longer fails correct items as wrong answers; the human review page shows the answer as the correct option ("Option 3: …") with options numbered from 1.
- Dark theme: mastered-topic checkmarks on the map use `--color-text-on-gold` (dark on gold) instead of the light body text, which gave about 1.3:1 contrast; the theme contrast tests now cover this pair and check the map rule (J8 follow-up).
- Flaky web tests (Q): the 5-a-day and recall check tests used findBy's 1 s default to wait for data that loads after the page, which ran out under CI load; they now allow 5 s, the mocked fetches are made slower than 1 s on purpose, and the "card is hidden" checks wait until the 5-a-day has actually answered. The Learn it first waits allow 5 s too.
- Flaky web test (I5): the unlock test waited for the student button but not for the parent sign-in buttons, which load after the provider list; it now waits for both, with the provider list made slow on purpose.
- Operator bridge (O, review follow-up to #71/#72): a change retried with the same request id by a different operator or role is refused (409) instead of getting the stored answer; the audit log's `nextBefore` is now `<time>~<id>`, so entries that share a time are never skipped between pages.
- Game feel (J): flashcard text was white on white inside the arena panel on the Cards tab; light cards inside the arena now keep dark text (`design/arena.css`). In a lesson, saying whether you knew a flashcard no longer hides the answer you had just revealed (the shake wrapper no longer remounts the card).
- Public pages (I): without a linked student on the device, `AppLayout` pages (About, Feedback, Settings and the rest) and the policy pages (now under `AppLayout`) show `PublicHeader` (logo, Sign in, Policies, About) instead of the student nav.
- No third-party requests (I): the deployed smoke test and the local e2e suite fail if a page requests any host other than the web origin and its API host. On staging it finds Cloudflare's zone-injected Web Analytics beacon (`static.cloudflareinsights.com`), which the owner is switching off in the Cloudflare dashboard.
- Policies: the privacy notice and services page (both now 1.1) say parents sign in with Google or Microsoft, whichever this app offers (a deployment may offer only one).
- Google and Microsoft access, refresh and ID tokens are no longer stored after sign-in (B2).
- Year group is limited to Years 9 to 13 in the API, the core schema and the database (B2, migration 0006).
- Students' own flashcards get the same personal-details check as feedback (B2, cleared with F).
- Stale app after a deploy (I): page visits skip the browser cache and refresh the offline copy (service worker v2); builds publish `/version.json` (`version-commit`); `UpdateBanner` checks it on start, on return to the foreground and every 15 minutes and offers Refresh; an unknown-route 404 or a wrong-shape reply from the API triggers a check and one automatic reload per new build (no loops).
- "Who's revising?" is parent view only (I, product owner decision): a child opens `/parent/stats?student=<id>` (preselected), never `/map`; per child "Rewards you set" and "Link a device" (`/onboarding?link=<id>` opens that child's link step); "Family feedback" link.
- Web app talks to the real API (I): the core client used placeholder paths the API never had (`family`, `students/:id/map`, `lessons/...`) and no cookies, so `/family` failed on staging after sign-in. It now reads `GET /v1/onboarding/family` (shared `OnboardingFamilySchema`) with `credentials: 'include'`; the welcome page starts Better Auth sign-in (`POST /api/auth/sign-in/social`, back to `/family`) instead of jumping to `/family`; a 401 on any screen (and onboarding) shows "Please sign in" with the provider buttons, returning to the same page; a family with no children goes from `/family` to `/onboarding`; sign-out (`POST /api/auth/sign-out`) on the welcome page and `/family`; the welcome page reads `GET /api/auth/get-session` and offers Continue and Sign out when signed in. Placeholder lesson screens removed (`/lesson/:id` opens `/learn/topic/:id`). Map and cards-due call F2's agreed `GET /v1/student/map` and `GET /v1/student/cards/due` (not on the API yet).
- Parent stats dashboard: a day with only flashcard practice no longer counts as a rest day (K).
- Opening My stats, the parent dashboard or a child's parent-set rewards no longer uses up the "New!" rewards the student should see on their Rewards page (K, via a read-only `rewardsProgress` in R's rewards routes).
- GCSE Statistics offers only AQA and Pearson Edexcel in onboarding (checked on the board websites).
- Verification protocol now covers extended answers (blind marking of exemplars plus individual human approval).
