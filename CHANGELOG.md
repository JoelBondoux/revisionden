# Changelog

All notable changes are recorded here. Versions use the format `xx.xx.xxx` (major.minor.patch).
Every push to `main` bumps the version automatically; add entries under **Unreleased** in each PR.

## [Unreleased]

### What's new

- Tap any power to switch between x² and x^2 (tap again to switch back). It changes every power on the screen, and your device remembers which you prefer.
- On the topic map, going past your daily goal now says "Goal smashed!" with a star, instead of an odd fraction like 195 / 50. The subject and unit rows also open with every visible chip whole, instead of one cut off at the left edge.
- Parent home and the Policies page now use big, chunky buttons with an icon and a short name, instead of long rows of text. Each effect style gives them its own look (for example a hand-drawn sticker in Doodle Diary, glossy candy in Pop Burst, an 8-bit block in Pixel Arcade), and the page they open slides in with that style's own move, which plays backwards when you go back. Get help still opens straight away, and nothing moves if your device asks for reduced motion.
- Powers now look the way you learn them in class: x² instead of x^2, everywhere in questions, flashcards and answers. Typing an answer? Tap the new Power key, and a preview shows how your answer looks. (Computing keeps the ^ symbol, as it means something there.)
- After a wrong answer, and on a flashcard's Explain, a "Learn more" button can now open free lessons on that exact question or skill from Oak National Academy, BBC Bitesize or Isaac. Every link was opened and checked to teach the right thing at the GCSE level, and there are now links for every GCSE Maths topic and skill with questions.
- Behind the scenes: questions made by our maths engine from an approved recipe now need fewer spot checks by a person once the recipe itself has been approved, so new practice questions can arrive faster; every automatic check still runs.
- Flashcards work better with screen readers: the card and the rating buttons now read out the words you can see.
- A shorter topic map that fits on one phone screen. Today's goal, your next mission and your 5-a-day are now in one "Today" box at the top. Below it, "Your path" shows your units as a row of badges (swipe for more), and the unit you pick shows all of its topics down a path you can scroll inside the card. Subjects stay on one line too.
- The stats pages feel livelier: the big numbers count up and the charts grow in when you first see them. It's quick and calm, it switches off if your device asks for reduced motion, and on My stats a soft sound plays when the numbers settle. Both stats pages also fit small phones with large text without scrolling sideways.
- Every Number question, and the pilot questions, now has a short clue that points you to the method without giving the answer away.
- We have worked out how to make verified questions for every subject, not just maths, and which subjects can be built fastest.
- A new **Me** button at the end of the menu shows your avatar and opens your settings and personalisation. Get help and your progress have moved there: Get help is the first thing on the page (just as private, nothing is recorded), with My progress right after it. The menu is now Map, Cards, Plan, Papers and Me, and Get help is no longer at the top and bottom of every screen.
- Setting up a passkey on your child's phone is more reliable: if it doesn't work, the page now says why (for example, the phone needs a screen lock, or the link was opened inside another app and should be opened in Chrome), and "Try again" works even after a few minutes or a page reload.
- On small phones with large text, the policy list and other link cards now wrap their words instead of running off the side of the screen.
- Fixed: the About page no longer scrolls sideways on a small phone with large text; the version details and support email now wrap.

- Fixed: when the version line at the bottom of a phone screen wraps, Get help now simply moves to the next line, with no stray dot at the start or end of either line.
